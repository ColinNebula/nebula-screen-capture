# 🎨 Creator Tools - Quick Start Guide

## What Are Creator Tools?

Creator Tools is a comprehensive suite of professional assets built into the Nebula Screen Capture video editor. It includes:

- 📚 **Templates** - Pre-made intros, outros, lower thirds, and transitions
- 🎨 **Stickers** - 200+ emojis, icons, shapes, and callouts
- 🎵 **Audio Library** - Music tracks, sound effects, and voiceovers

---

## How to Access

### Step 1: Open Video Editor
Record or select a video, then click "Edit" to open the video editor.

### Step 2: Find Creator Tools Tab
Look for the **"Creator Tools"** button in the editor tabs. It has a special gradient background with a grid icon.

### Step 3: Click to Open
Click the Creator Tools button to open the full-screen modal interface.

---

## Using Templates

### Browse Templates
1. The Templates tab opens by default
2. See categories: Intros, Outros, Lower Thirds, Transitions, Text
3. Use the search bar to find specific templates

### Preview & Apply
- Click **"Preview"** (👁️) to see a demo
- Click **"Use"** (✨) to apply to your video
- Premium templates are marked with 👑 PRO badge

### Template Types

**Intros (3-5 seconds)**
- Modern Intro - Sleek logo reveal
- Minimal Intro - Clean text fade
- Tech Intro - Futuristic style [PRO]

**Outros (4-6 seconds)**
- Subscribe Outro - CTA screen
- Thanks Outro - Thank you message
- Social Media - Multi-platform links [PRO]

**Lower Thirds**
- Display names and titles
- Persistent or timed
- Various styles available

## Using Stickers

### Browse Stickers
1. Click the **"Stickers"** tab
2. Choose a category: Emojis, Arrows, Shapes, Icons, etc.
3. Search by name or tag

### Add to Video
**Method 1: Drag & Drop**
- Click and drag sticker onto video preview
- Position where you want it
- Release to place

**Method 2: Quick Add**
- Click the sticker once
- It adds at center position
- Adjust position later

### Sticker Categories

**😊 Emojis**
- Reactions: 👍 👎 🔥 ❤️ ⭐ 😂
- Expressions: 🤔 😱 😎
- Use for emphasis and engagement

**→ Arrows**
- All directions: ← → ↑ ↓
- Special: ↪ ⇒ ➡️
- Guide viewer attention

**⬛ Shapes**
- Basic: Circle, Square, Triangle
- Special: Diamond, Hexagon [PRO]
- For highlights and indicators

**📌 Icons**
- Actions: ✓ ✗ 🔔 📷 🎥
- Objects: ⚡ 🏆 🚀 🎯 💡
- Add professional polish

**ℹ️ Callouts**
- Info, Warning, Error, Success
- Question, Exclamation
- Draw attention to important info

**✨ Decorations**
- Sparkles, Crown [PRO], Gem [PRO]
- Boom, Party, 100
- Make content pop

## Using Audio

### Browse Audio
1. Click the **"Audio"** tab
2. Choose category: Music, SFX, Voiceover
3. See BPM, duration, and mood

### Preview & Add
- Click **▶️ Play** to hear preview
- Adjust volume slider before adding
- Click **"Add"** (➕) to add to timeline
- Track appears in Now Playing with progress bar

### Audio Categories

**🎼 Music**

## Tips & Tricks

### ⚡ Quick Tips

1. **Search Everything**
   - Every tab has a search bar
   - Search by name, tag, or mood
   - Filters as you type

2. **Premium Features**
   - Free tier: 80% of assets
   - Premium: 20% advanced assets
   - Marked with 👑 PRO badge

3. **Keyboard Shortcuts**
   - `ESC` - Close Creator Tools
   - `Ctrl/Cmd + F` - Focus search (planned)

4. **Responsive Design**
   - Works on desktop and tablet
   - Mobile optimized (limited)
   - Adapts to screen size

### 🎨 Best Practices

**For Professional Videos:**
- Use consistent template style
- Limit stickers (3-5 per video max)
- Match audio to video mood
- Don't overuse callouts

**For Engagement:**
- Add subscribe sticker in outros
- Use reaction emojis sparingly
- Include social media icons
- Upbeat music for energy

**For Tutorials:**
- Use arrows to point at elements
- Add checkmarks for steps
- Info icons for tips
- Clear, simple stickers

## Troubleshooting

### Stickers Not Appearing?
- Check if video preview is visible
- Try clicking instead of dragging
- Refresh editor if needed

### Audio Not Playing?
- Audio preview uses placeholder URLs
- Full implementation coming soon
- Check browser console for errors

### Templates Not Applying?
- Template application is in development
- Currently shows preview only
- Use as reference for manual editing

### Performance Issues?
- Close other browser tabs
- Reduce sticker count
- Disable preview animations
