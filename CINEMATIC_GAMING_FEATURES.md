## 📚 Industry Standards to Follow

### Color Management
- **Rec.709** - HDTV standard
- **Rec.2020** - UHDTV/4K HDR
- **DCI-P3** - Digital cinema
- **sRGB** - Web standard

### Frame Rates
- **23.976 fps** - Film standard
- **24 fps** - True cinema
- **25 fps** - PAL (Europe)
- **29.97 fps** - NTSC (Americas)
- **30 fps** - Web video
- **60 fps** - Sports, gaming

### Aspect Ratios
- **16:9** - Standard widescreen
- **21:9** - Ultra-widescreen
- **2.39:1** - Anamorphic cinema
- **1.85:1** - Theatrical
- **4:3** - Classic TV/vintage
- **9:16** - Vertical (mobile)
