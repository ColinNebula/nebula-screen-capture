# 🎬 Multi-Track Sequencer Features

## Overview
The timeline has been transformed into a professional multi-track sequencer with drag-and-drop functionality, similar to Adobe Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve.

## ✨ New Features

### 1. **Multi-Track Timeline**
- **Video Tracks**: Up to 4+ video layers with full compositing
- **Audio Tracks**: Dedicated audio tracks with independent controls
- **Effects Tracks**: Special tracks for effects and overlays
- **Dynamic Track Addition**: Add/remove tracks on the fly

### 2. **Visual Clip Management**
- **Clip Blocks**: Visual representation of clips with thumbnails
- **Color Coding**: Auto-assigned colors for easy identification
- **Clip Information**: Duration and name labels on each clip
- **Thumbnail Backgrounds**: Blurred video thumbnails for context

### 3. **Drag & Drop Editing**
- **Free Positioning**: Drag clips anywhere on the timeline
- **Magnetic Snapping**: Clips snap to each other and the playhead
- **Cross-Track Movement**: Move clips between tracks
- **Visual Feedback**: Clips highlight and show shadow while dragging

### 4. **Track Controls**
Each track has professional controls:
- **Mute (M)**: Silence the track
- **Solo (S)**: Play only this track
- **Lock (L)**: Prevent accidental edits
- **Track Height**: Adjustable for detailed editing

### 5. **Advanced Timeline Features**
- **Timeline Ruler**: Precise time markers every second
- **Grid Lines**: Visual guides for alignment
- **Playhead**: Red playhead with time display
- **Zoom Controls**: 25%-400% zoom for precision

### 6. **Clip Operations**
- **Split at Playhead**: Double-click any clip to split
- **Delete Clips**: X button on hover
- **Resize Handles**: Trim clip in/out points
- **Selection**: Yellow border indicates selected clip

### 7. **Professional Workflow**
- **Magnetic Snapping**: Toggle 🧲 for automatic alignment
- **Ripple Edit**: Toggle 🌊 for automatic gap closing
- **Snap to Frames**: Toggle 📍 for frame-accurate edits

## 🎯 Usage

### Adding Clips to Sequencer
1. Use the **Import Video** button in the outliner
2. Clips automatically appear on the first video track
3. Drag clips to rearrange or move to other tracks

### Creating Tracks
1. Click **Video**, **Audio**, or **FX** buttons in the toolbar
2. New track appears at the bottom
3. Each track is independent with its own controls

### Editing Clips
- **Move**: Click and drag clip to new position
- **Split**: Double-click clip at playhead position
- **Delete**: Click X button while hovering
- **Resize**: Drag left/right edges (coming soon)
- **Select**: Click to select, shows yellow border

### Track Management
- **Mute**: Click speaker icon to mute track
- **Solo**: Click S to solo (only play this track)
- **Lock**: Click lock icon to prevent editing

### Navigation
- **Zoom**: Use 🔍+/- buttons or scroll wheel
- **Scroll**: Pan horizontally to see more timeline
- **Playhead**: Click anywhere on time ruler to jump

## 🎨 Visual Indicators

- **Blue Clips**: Video 1 track
- **Purple/Pink Clips**: Video 2 track
- **Green/Cyan Clips**: Audio tracks
- **Orange/Red Clips**: Effects tracks
- **Yellow Border**: Selected clip
- **Red Playhead**: Current position
- **Grid Lines**: Second markers

## 🚀 Keyboard Shortcuts (Coming Soon)

- `Space`: Play/Pause
- `I`: Set In Point
- `O`: Set Out Point
- `M`: Mute selected track
- `S`: Solo selected track
- `L`: Lock selected track
- `Delete`: Remove selected clip
- `Cmd/Ctrl + D`: Duplicate clip
- `Cmd/Ctrl + Z`: Undo
- `Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + Z`: Redo

## 🔄 Import Workflow

1. **Outliner Panel**: Import multiple videos
2. **Clips appear**: On first video track automatically
3. **Arrange**: Drag clips to desired positions
4. **Layer**: Move clips between tracks for layering
5. **Edit**: Apply filters, effects, transitions per clip
6. **Export**: Render final composition

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Use magnetic snapping** for quick alignment
2. **Color-code clips** by track for organization
3. **Lock tracks** you're not editing to avoid accidents
4. **Zoom in** for frame-accurate trimming
5. **Use multiple video tracks** for picture-in-picture
6. **Split clips** to apply different effects to segments
7. **Solo tracks** to preview isolated elements
