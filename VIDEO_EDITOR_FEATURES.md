# 🎬 Professional Video Editor - Complete Feature List

## Overview
The Video Editor now includes professional-grade editing capabilities with 6 comprehensive tabs.

---

## 📋 **Tab 1: Trim & Timeline**

### Features:
- ✂️ **Visual Timeline** - Interactive timeline with draggable handles
- 🎯 **Precise Trimming** - Click anywhere to seek, drag handles to trim
- ⏱️ **Real-time Display** - Shows current time, duration, and percentage
- 🎮 **Playback Controls** - Play/pause, jump to start/end
- ⌨️ **Keyboard Shortcuts**:
  - `Space` - Play/Pause
  - `←/→` - Skip backward/forward 1 second
  - `ESC` - Close editor

---

## 🎨 **Tab 2: Filters & Color Correction**

### Basic Adjustments:
- ☀️ **Brightness** (0-200%) - Lighten or darken the video
- 🔆 **Contrast** (0-200%) - Adjust the difference between light and dark
- 🌈 **Saturation** (0-200%) - Control color intensity
- 💫 **Blur** (0-10px) - Apply blur effect
- 🎨 **Hue Rotate** (0-360°) - Shift colors around the color wheel

### Advanced Color Correction:
- 🌡️ **Temperature** (-100 to +100)
  - Negative: Cool (blue tones)
  - Positive: Warm (orange tones)
- 🎨 **Tint** (-100 to +100)
  - Negative: Green tones
  - Positive: Magenta tones
- ☀️ **Exposure** (-100 to +100) - Brightness with better highlight control

### Controls:
- 🔄 **Reset All** - Reset all filters to default

---

## ✨ **Tab 3: Effects**

### Zoom & Pan:
- 🔍 **Zoom** (1x to 3x) - Digital zoom into video
- ↔️ **Horizontal Position** - Pan left/right when zoomed
- ↕️ **Vertical Position** - Pan up/down when zoomed

### Artistic Effects:
- 🎭 **Vignette** (0-100%) - Darken edges for cinematic look
- ✨ **Sharpen** (0-100%) - Enhance detail and clarity

### Controls:
- 🔄 **Reset All** - Reset all effects to default

---

## 🎞️ **Tab 4: Transitions**

### Fade In:
- 📈 **Duration** (0-5 seconds) - Length of fade in effect
- 🎨 **Type**:
  - From Black - Classic fade from black
  - From White - Bright fade from white
  - From Transparent - Fade in from transparency

### Fade Out:
- 📉 **Duration** (0-5 seconds) - Length of fade out effect
- 🎨 **Type**:
  - To Black - Classic fade to black
  - To White - Bright fade to white
  - To Transparent - Fade to transparency

### Controls:
- 🔄 **Reset All** - Reset all transitions to default

---

## 🔊 **Tab 5: Audio & Playback**

### Volume Control:
- 🔊 **Volume** (0-100%) - Master volume level
- 🎚️ **Audio Normalization** - Automatically balance audio levels
- 🎵 **Audio Enhancement** - Improve overall audio quality

### Playback Speed:
- ⏩ **Speed** (0.25x to 2x) - Slow motion or fast forward
- **Quick Presets**: 0.5x, 0.75x, 1x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 2x

### Controls:
- 🔄 **Reset** - Reset audio settings to default

---

## 🚀 **Pro Tips**

1. **Preview Before Export**: Test all settings with playback before exporting
2. **Use Keyboard Shortcuts**: Faster workflow with Space and arrow keys
3. **Layered Adjustments**: Combine filters, effects, and transitions for unique looks
4. **Zoom + Pan**: Create dynamic camera movements with zoom controls
5. **Audio Normalization**: Enable for consistent volume across different clips
6. **Watermark Placement**: Use opacity to make watermarks subtle
7. **Transition Timing**: Keep fade durations under 2 seconds for professional look
8. **Color Grading Workflow**:
   - Start with Exposure
   - Adjust Brightness/Contrast
   - Fine-tune with Temperature/Tint
   - Add Saturation last
